2. Risk Assessment

Low Risk

  • > 32 week gestation
  • > 1500 g birth weight

Moderate Risk

  • VLBW 1000-1500g
  • Congenital cardiac disease
  • Inotropic support

High Risk

  • < 28 weeks gestation or < 1000g
  • Surgical cases / Post-NEC
3. Initiations and Advancement

Start 60-90 mL/kg/day boluses.
Advance 30-40 mL/kg/day.

Mod-High Risk Path

Trophic feeds (10-20 mL/kg/day).
Advance 20-30 mL/kg/day.
