2. Risk Assessment

Low Risk

  • > 32 week gestation
  • > 1500 g birth weight
  • No other risk factors

Moderate Risk

  • 28+1 - 31+6 gestation or Very Low Birth Weight 1000-1500g
  • Complex congenital cardiac disease
  • Dexamethasone treatment
  • Indomethacin or Ibuprofen treatment for PDA
  • Inotropic support
  • Symptomatic polycythaemia
  • Previous NEC

High Risk

  • < 28 weeks gestation or Extremely Low Birth Weight < 1000g
  • Absent or reversed end diastolic flow in infants < 34 weeks
  • Perinatal hypoxia-ischaemia with organ dysfunction
  • Hypotensive / unstable ventilated neonates
  • Commencing feeds after an episode of NEC or gastrointestinal surgery
3. Initiations and Advancement

Low Risk Path

Step 1

Introduce buccal colostrum as soon as possible after birth.

Step 2

Start full enteral feeds 60-90 mL/kg/day as 2 to 3 hourly boluses as soon as possible.

Step 3

Advance feeds as tolerated 30-40 mL/kg/day.

Step 4

Continue to monitor feed tolerance regularly with cares.

Moderate - High Risk Path

First 24 Hours

Start by colostrum - start TPN.

24 - 48 Hours

Continue colostrum as available until sufficient MBM is supplied to start trophic feeds (10 - 20 ml / kg /day).

At 48 Hours

If no MBM, discuss donor breastmilk (DBM) or baby formula with parents.

Advancement

20 - 30 ml /kg / day. Aim to decrease PN only once baby on total fluids of 150ml/kg/day.
