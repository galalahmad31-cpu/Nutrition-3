Clinical Reference

Quick references for neonatal parenteral and enteral nutrition.

  • Glucose Infusion Rate (GIR): Target 4-8 mg/kg/min initially.
  • Protein: Start at 2-3 g/kg/day, advance to 3.5-4 g/kg/day.
  • Lipids: Start at 1-2 g/kg/day, advance by 0.5-1 g/kg/day to max 3 g/kg/day.
